To judge a haunted receipt, in three minutes.

Cook Mode for one handwritten recipe from c. 1750: at each step where the writer left a judgement, such as “till it Looks red & smooth”, steam fogs a window and the writer's own scanned ink is wiped clear of it, quoted from the archive, never generated.

i · see it working

i.The 60-second path

One link, five presses. The pot runs at a visible ×12 pace (the app's own pace, shown in the pane), so the step arrives in seconds instead of minutes.

  1. 0:00 · open

    Open the hero deep link.

    Cook Mode opens on step 5 of 5, …boil it till it Looks red & smooth…, with the pot under a night window.

  2. ~0:06 · watch

    Wait about 6 s, or press Show the note.

    The fog thickens and the writer's note is written out of it in the scan's own ink, about 5 s of writing (WRITE_ON_MS in src/steam/timeline.ts). The typed step stays on screen under the glass.

  3. ~0:15 · press

    Press The hand, quoted.

    The glass wipes and a page opens with every note cut from the scan, each with its shelfmark and licence.

  4. ~0:25 · check

    Press verify at source ↗ under a note.

    The note's bounding box opens on Wellcome Collection's own image server (it may take in a little of the neighbouring lines).

  5. ~0:40 · read

    Open the edition, then Read the provenance.

    The transcription marks with † each of the 5 places where the two Kiro readers disagree, both readings shown; the slip expands to institution, shelfmark, licence and IIIF manifest.

  6. optional

    On step 1 (?note=full-ripe) drag across the glass, or press Wipe the glass.

    The fog closes and Cook Mode moves to the next step. Offline: after one visit, turn the network off and open the link again; it runs from the service worker's cache.

The hero deep link, to paste: https://steam.edycu.dev/#/r/quinces-marmalade/cook?note=till-it-looks-red-smooth&pace=12&lead=6every step with what to expect, the other deep links and the reduced-motion behaviour: DEMO.md

ii · where each criterion is answered

ii.The five criteria

Each row: what we claim, where to check it, and the limit that bounds it.

Potential Impacta real problem, a real audience

Typing a recipe up keeps the words but loses the hand and the page they came from. For anyone who would rather cook from, or check a transcription against, the writer's own hand: every note links back to its region of the archive's scan. Bounded: one recipe, no upload.

  • README: the problem and the solution
  • Steps 4 and 5 above: verify at source, the † edition
  • Accessibility: e2e/cook-a11y.spec.ts, Lighthouse a11y 100

Technological Implementationhow deeply Kiro was used

Specs, bugfix specs, six hooks, steering, two isolated custom agents and a workflow with a code gate. Then we measured our own hooks and found they saw about 5% of the writes, which became a bugfix spec.

  • Section iii below, each feature linked to its file
  • docs/KIRO.md: the causal chain
  • EXPERIMENTS.md, DX-REPORT.md

Designa complete, intentional product

The haunting is the interface: the fog is the cue, the lamp dip draws the eye, wiping the glass says “seen” and moves on. Shelf → edition → Cook Mode → the hand, quoted → the archive. Bounded: the renderer's last scored look pass was 6.5/10 against its own 7/10 gate.

Innovation / Ideanovel, public resources combined

A public-domain manuscript from Wellcome Collection's IIIF server becomes the UI: the writing is the scan's own ink, never a font or a model. Two Kiro readers disagree in the open (†) instead of a confident guess.

iii · technological implementation

iii.Kiro, file by file

Every card links to the file in the public repo. The builder was a Claude agent that wrote the prompts for the 26 build sessions; the other 13 sessions (workflow readers, hook and permission self-tests, one aborted) were launched from inside them. Kiro wrote the specs and most of the code, tests and fixes; the builder committed the rest directly, labelled [claude-direct]. The full chain, feature → files → commits → how to check: docs/KIRO.md.

The finding we did not want · bugfix spec hook-coverage

Our hooks saw about 5% of the writes.

Our write hooks matched only Kiro's write tools. Kiro wrote mostly through its shell tool. By the session ledger's write rule, a heuristic count of commands, not a filesystem audit, there were 30 write-tool calls under src/ (the only kind the hooks matched) against 543 flagged shell commands, and none of the 543 had a hook event. 12 of the 30 and 172 of the 543 came before the hooks existed; from the session that installed them on, it is 18 against 371, still about 5%.

vi.Real or simulated

The writing on the glass is the scan's own ink

real

Lifted off the scan and drawn in the renderer's colour: a processed render checked at region level (NCC, and the ink's bounding box lies inside the verified region), not a pixel-level proof.

The page is Wellcome Collection MS.1810, p.23

real

Fetched through the IIIF Image API; “verify at source” opens the region on Wellcome's own server.

Cook Mode runs offline after one visit

tested

Service-worker precache: verify_offline.py passed on a production deploy (verify-offline-live.txt); its no-worker negative control, which cannot pass, was run on a local build (verify-offline.txt).

The pot's timing

sped up, labelled

×12 is the app's own pace parameter, badged in the pane. The manuscript gives no cooking times, so none is invented.

The order the strokes are written in

ours

The write-on follows a traced path through the ink, not the writer's pen order.

The phone shot in the video

emulated

A phone-size viewport in Chrome, no handset.

A human signed off the reading

not yet

The sign-off record is the builder's agent, and the edition says so.

Bring your own family card

not built

No upload or import flow; family cards are the direction, not a demonstrated flow.

vii · bounded, said plainly

vii.Honest limits

  • One recipe is on the shelf.The ingestion pipeline has run on that one page only. A judge cannot bring their own card.
  • The renderer missed its own look gate.6.5/10 desktop and 6.5 phone at its last scored pass (look pass 2), against a 7/10 gate; the shipped pass 3 was not scored.
  • The reading's sign-off is the builder's agent, not a person.A human sign-off is still owed.
  • Our hooks were blind to most writes until we measured them.The 543 is a heuristic count of shell commands, not a filesystem audit; shell-gate does not see a write by a script run by name, or a path built from parts, before it runs; src-scan-after-shell reports it after.
  • Kiro ran headless through kiro-cli.IDE-only features (Spec Correctness, checkpoints, PostTaskExec hooks) were not used, and no build-wide credit total was kept.
  • Not built: an MCP server, a Power, an archivist agent, the fog A/B and the spec-vs-vibe comparison.All stated in docs/KIRO.md section 4.
