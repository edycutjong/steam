02 · hookHer Hand in the Steam
Handwritten recipes keep the judgement calls a timer cannot. This line is the cook's real test for when the quinces are done. We bring that judgement back at the moment it matters.

Typed recipes lose the judgement.

Typing a recipe up flattens “till it looks red & smooth” into a line of text, and the person who wrote it disappears.

Who cooks from it: the inheritor, at a stove at night, hands wet.
